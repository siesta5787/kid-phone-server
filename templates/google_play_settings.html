Can't use a phone? Sign in manually
  1. On a computer, open a private browser window and go to https://accounts.google.com/EmbeddedSetup.
  2. Sign in with the spare account and accept the terms if asked. The page may end up blank or spinning - that's fine.
  3. Open the browser's developer tools (F12) → Application/Storage → Cookies → accounts.google.com, and copy the value of the oauth_token cookie (it starts with oauth2_4/).
  4. Paste it below within a few minutes - it only works once.
