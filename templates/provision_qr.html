Provision a new device

Works on stock Android and most custom ROMs via the standard 6-tap QR provisioning flow. Does not currently work on GrapheneOS - its setup wizard doesn't have this hook built in yet (tracked upstream, not something we can fix here). For GrapheneOS, keep using the adb-based setup instead.

How to use it

  1. Factory reset the device (or use one fresh out of the box).
  2. On the Welcome screen, tap the same spot 6 times in a row.
  3. Scan the QR code below when prompted.
  4. Follow the on-screen setup - it downloads and installs the launcher and sets it as device owner automatically.
  5. Once it boots into the launcher, open Settings and enter a device enrollment code from that device's page here, same as any other device.

WiFi (optional)

Fill this in to have the device join your network automatically during setup, before it downloads the launcher. Leave blank to connect manually during setup instead.
