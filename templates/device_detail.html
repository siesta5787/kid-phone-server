Quick Controls (swipe-left screen)

These appear on a screen the kid can reach by swiping left from the home screen. This is the intended way for a kid to reach these controls instead of Android's own Quick Settings shade, which can't be fully locked down - a third-party app can publish its own tile there (Tailscale does) that bypasses restrictions below.

Ad/content filtering (VPN)

The launcher runs its own on-device VPN to filter ads/content locally (see the DNS/Filters tab for blocklists and what's been blocked). Turning this off disables that filtering on this device entirely - the device still syncs normally, it just stops blocking anything. There's no on-device way for a kid to turn this off themselves.

Offline override PIN

A failsafe for when the phone can't reach the server at all. Entering this PIN directly on the phone temporarily lifts every restriction; it reasserts automatically the moment the phone can sync again.
